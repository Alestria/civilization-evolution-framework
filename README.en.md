# Civilization Differentiation and Integration Philosophy

[中文首页](README.md)

How do societies, organizations, and technical communities develop different roles while continuing to work together? When does dependence give one party power over another? This project develops a framework for studying these questions, from the way teams divide work to the way global networks respond to disruption.

## Two lines of research

- **Framework development** clarifies concepts, compares them with existing research, and revises models in light of evidence.
- **Exploratory studies** begin with a specific problem. Each study aims to leave a record of what was found, how it was established, and what is still uncertain. Its findings should remain useful even if the framework changes.

## Current status

The [Chinese V0.4.3 blueprint](docs/v0.4.3.md) is the reference for definitions, models, notation, and shared research methods. See the [freeze record](FREEZE.md) for what was signed off. Hypotheses H1–H9 have yet to be independently tested. The case files document pilot work and what the available sources can support.
